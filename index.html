The Royals — NGO Website

Donations & Awareness Platform · Stanix Consulting

ROLE — PROJECT MANAGER
TIMELINE — 6 WEEKS
YEAR — 2024–PRESENT
6 wksDelivery Window
2Site Goals: Donations + Awareness
Situation
The Royals needed a single website to serve two goals at once: driving donations and raising awareness of its programs, with no PRD or delivery plan yet in place.
Task
Own delivery end-to-end within a 6-week window, and keep the project's scope from drifting as new requests came in.
Action
Partnered directly with the Product Manager to shape the PRD and define scope up front. Coordinated design and development work against that scope, ran regular check-ins to catch misalignment early, and pushed back on out-of-scope requests to protect the timeline and the team's focus.
Result
Delivered on schedule within 6 weeks, with a live site supporting both the donation and awareness/program goals it was scoped for.
