3PLE Hotel, Ikorodu — Solar Power Installation

20kWp Solar & Battery System · Cesol3nergy

ROLE — TECHNICAL PROJECT MANAGER
TIMELINE — 9 WKS (ORIG. PLAN: 5)
YEAR — 2023–2024
20kWp / 45kWhPanel & Battery Capacity
0Critical Defects, Portfolio Close-Out
Situation
System was scoped and priced for one building. Mid-project, the client asked to split it across a second hotel property, which turned out to sit on a different transformer, and the inverter already purchased had no pairing function to safely run two sources as one system.
Task
Find the root cause fast, protect the budget and schedule, and keep a crew that had been pushing hard for weeks from burning out before the job closed.
Action
Escalated to Cesol3nergy leadership and brought in an independent electrical engineer to inspect the site. Confirmed the transformer mismatch and inverter limitation as the cause, redesigned each hotel building as its own independent system, drew on the company's reserve budget to cover the damaged equipment and rework, and arranged incentive pay to keep the crew motivated through the extension.
Result
Delivered a correctly split, working system, one inverter setup per building. Client was impressed at handover by how the issue was handled. Job closed as part of a ten-project portfolio that finished with zero critical defects across the board.

Lilly Estate, Festac — Solar CCTV Deployment

60+ Camera Security Network · Cesol3nergy

ROLE — TECHNICAL PROJECT MANAGER
TIMELINE — 7 WKS (ORIG. PLAN: 4)
YEAR — 2023–2024
60+Cameras Deployed
1Centralised Feed, Re-Engineered Mid-Project
Situation
Estate initially asked for per-resident mobile viewing, and cameras were bought for that. Mid-install, the estate switched to wanting all cameras monitored from a single screen by security staff instead, which the purchased system had no built-in way to do.
Task
Get the hardware and software to support centralised monitoring, and physically wire 60-plus cameras into a single hub after the original install crew had already rolled onto other jobs.
Action
Renegotiated with the camera supplier for a centralised-monitoring configuration. Followed up closely on site, personally, to keep the rewiring on track, and ran the new cabling underground for a cleaner finish. Revised the budget directly with the client to reflect the change in scope.
Result
Estate security got single-source monitoring, the wiring came out neater than the original above-ground plan, and the client signed off satisfied despite the mid-project detour.

Link-O — Escalation Protocol Redesign

Service Operations · Link-O

ROLE — OPERATIONS PROJECT LEAD
TIMELINE — NOV 2020 – DEC 2022
YEAR — 2020–2022
12%Faster Average Resolution Time
96%Weekly Resolution Rate (100+ Enquiries)
Situation
A service operation handling 100-plus enquiries a week had no clear escalation path, so issues sat with the wrong owner and resolution times drifted.
Task
Fix the workflow driving that pattern and give the team visibility into its own performance.
Action
Redesigned the escalation protocol so ownership was clear at every stage, rather than sitting with whoever picked an issue up first, and built performance dashboards to track resolution and reporting accuracy.
Result
Average resolution time dropped 12%, the team sustained a 96% resolution rate, and reporting accuracy improved 15%.
